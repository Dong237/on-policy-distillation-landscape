# Entropy and Uncertainty

Distillation objectives shape student uncertainty.

## Common patterns

- Forward KL can encourage mode covering.
- Reverse KL can encourage mode seeking.
- Hard CE can collapse uncertainty early.
- Teacher uncertainty should not be blindly copied.

## Track

- Objective type.
- Temperature.
- Whether teacher probabilities are filtered or calibrated.
- Whether the method distinguishes confident and uncertain teacher states.
