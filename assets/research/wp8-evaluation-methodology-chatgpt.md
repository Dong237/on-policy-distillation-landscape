## Evaluation Philosophy

OPD evaluation should test whether teacher-style supervision on the student's own states changes behavior in ways reward-only RLVR, offline KD, DPO/RLHF, or ordinary SFT do not. Static final-answer accuracy is necessary but not sufficient. Every serious OPD claim should report three layers:

1. Capability: accuracy, pass rate, grounding success, manipulation success, or human/judge win rate on the target task.
2. On-policy robustness: rollout drift, OOD prompt slices, recovery from self-generated mistakes, long-horizon credit assignment, and performance conditional on first-error states.
3. Distributional transfer: teacher/student KL or JS on student rollouts, entropy and calibration preservation, teacher disagreement slices, selective prediction, and cost per improved token/trajectory.

The best bundles for separating OPD gains from reward-only RLVR gains pair reward-friendly tasks with non-reward-only diagnostics. For text reasoning, this means MATH-500/AIME/LiveCodeBench plus IFEval, LiveBench, calibration, and self-correction overlays. For VLM reasoning, it means MathVista/MMMU-Pro/MathVision plus OCRBench/ChartQA/DocVQA and teacher-disagreement slices. For agents and robotics, it means task success plus state/action-level recovery after an induced mistake. For diffusion/flow borderline methods, it means image reward or human preference plus teacher-field fidelity and diversity/coverage metrics.

The benchmarks that actually test exposure-bias reduction or recovery from self-generated errors are interactive or trajectory-state benchmarks: OSWorld, WebArena, AppWorld, SWE-bench Verified, ScienceWorld/ALFWorld/WebShop, LIBERO/RoboTwin2.0, and GUI grounding/action tasks when evaluated with perturb-and-recover protocols. Long-context suites such as RULER, LongBench v2, and BABILong test context drift, but not self-generated error recovery unless a rerollout or corrupted-prefix protocol is layered on top. Single-shot math and VQA accuracy do not directly test exposure bias.

Teacher uncertainty preservation should be measured directly whenever teacher probabilities or action distributions are available. Report token/action KL, JS, entropy gap, top-k mass preservation, ECE, Brier/NLL, selective coverage-risk, teacher-student rank correlation, and calibration conditioned on teacher entropy. For black-box/API and discriminator methods, replace unavailable logit metrics with preference calibration, judge/discriminator calibration, reward margin stability, agreement under judge swaps, and reward hacking diagnostics.

## Method-Type Evaluation Rules

| method_type | evaluation emphasis | required controls | OPD-specific diagnostics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Token/logit OPD | Capability plus distributional fidelity on current student rollouts. | SFT; offline KD on fixed teacher traces; reward-only RLVR where applicable; DPO/RLHF if the target is alignment. | On-policy KL/JS; entropy gap; teacher disagreement slices; rollout freshness ablation; teacher-query budget; first-error recovery. |
| Response-level black-box OPD | Alignment quality and reward/judge robustness, because token-level distribution fidelity is unavailable. | RLAIF/RLHF reward-only; DPO/ORPO from API preferences; teacher-generated SFT; strict white-box OPD if teacher access can be approximated. | Judge-swap stability; discriminator calibration; pairwise win-rate confidence intervals; response length bias; adversarial reward hacking; preference margin calibration. |
| Privileged self-OPD | Whether the privileged view transfers without leaking answer/context at evaluation time. | Ordinary self-play preference methods; SFT on privileged traces; same-model no-privilege self-distillation; reward-only RLVR. | Privilege-removal ablation; token KL to privileged self-teacher; calibration before/after privilege transfer; recovery on states where the base view is initially wrong. |
| OPD+RL hybrids | Separate the teacher-distribution effect from the outcome-reward effect. | GRPO/PPO/RLVR-only; OPD-only without reward; SFT cold start; offline KD; DPO/IPO/SimPO when preference-alignment is relevant. | Two-factor ablation: reward on/off and teacher KL on/off; teacher-logit advantage magnitude; reward-KL conflict rate; accuracy on OOD and unverifiable slices. |
| Action-token OPD | State-action distribution matching and task success under compounding errors. | Behavior cloning; DAgger or expert-correction SFT; online RL success reward; offline imitation. | Action KL on visited states; recovery after bad action prefix; success by horizon length; safety violations; expert-query budget. |
| Diffusion/flow borderline OPD | Image quality plus trajectory/field fidelity, not just preference reward. | Offline diffusion distillation; consistency/LCM-style distillation; DDPO/reward fine-tuning; DPO-style image preference tuning. | Teacher velocity/score-field MSE on student states; trajectory KL proxy; diversity/coverage; prompt-compositional fidelity; reward overoptimization checks. |

## Metric Taxonomy Table

| metric_family | measures | applies_to | example_metrics | failure_modes_caught | limitations |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Task success and capability | Whether the method solves target tasks. | All modalities. | exact match; accuracy; pass@1/pass@k; task success; mIoU; ANLS; click accuracy; manipulation success; FID/KID. | Regression in headline capability; poor transfer to target task. | Accuracy-only metrics do not prove OPD, expose exposure bias, or measure distributional fidelity. |
| On-policy distributional fidelity | Whether the student matches teacher/expert behavior on student-generated states. | Token/logit OPD; privileged self-OPD; action-token OPD; diffusion/flow. | token KL; reverse KL; JS; TVD; action KL/NLL; teacher-student log-prob gap; velocity/score-field MSE; top-k mass agreement. | Teacher signal ignored; reward-only behavior; mode collapse; action drift. | Requires teacher probabilities or expert distributions; cross-tokenizer and black-box settings need approximations. |
| Teacher uncertainty preservation | Whether student preserves or corrects teacher uncertainty. | Entropy-aware OPD; calibration OPD; GUI-SD; Uni-OPD; VLA. | entropy gap; entropy rank correlation; ECE by teacher-entropy bin; Brier; NLL; top-k entropy; selective risk at coverage. | Hard-label collapse; overconfident wrong answers; underconfident correct answers; copied bad uncertainty. | Calibration depends on confidence extraction, temperature, prompt format, and multiple-choice normalization. |
| Rollout drift and exposure bias | Whether training handles states induced by the student policy. | Strict OPD; partial OPD; action/agent OPD. | performance by generation length; first-error position; drift distance from reference; recovery after corrupted prefix; replan success; invalid action rate. | Student explores states the teacher/offline data never covers; long-CoT drift; compounding errors. | Needs published perturbation protocol and seeds; can be expensive for agents/robotics. |
| Self-generated error recovery | Whether the model can identify and repair its own mistakes. | Teacher-free OPD; agent OPD; code/tool OPD; long-context OPD. | conditional improvement rate; regression rate; success after failed first attempt; patch repair rate; second-pass correctness. | Self-correction that only edits style; reward hacking; inability to recover from early wrong steps. | Static benchmarks rarely include this natively; overlays can reduce comparability. |
| Reward-distillation conflict | Whether reward and teacher signals disagree. | OPD+RL hybrids; black-box partial OPD; VLM RLVR controls. | reward-KL conflict rate; gradient cosine between reward and KL terms; accuracy on teacher-disagreement slice; KL-weight sweep. | Reward-only overoptimization; teacher suppresses exploration; KL prevents better-than-teacher gains. | Requires training instrumentation; not always reproducible from checkpoints. |
| Judge and discriminator robustness | Whether response-level supervision is stable. | Black-box/API OPD; PRISM; RLHF/RLAIF/DPO controls. | judge-swap agreement; win-rate CI; length-normalized win; discriminator ECE; adversarial prompt score; pairwise margin stability. | LLM-judge bias; discriminator reward hacking; response verbosity inflation. | Does not recover token-level distributional evidence; judge APIs can change. |
| Systems efficiency | Cost of producing improvement. | Industrial OPD; speculative OPD; all teacher-scored methods. | teacher calls/token; GPU-hours; tokens/s; TTFT; latency p50/p95; acceptance rate; quality per dollar; rollout throughput. | OPD gains that are uneconomical; cached/offline approximations masquerading as strict OPD. | Hardware and implementation sensitive; private workloads are hard to compare. |
| Safety and side effects | Whether on-policy exploration causes unsafe or invalid behavior. | Agentic OPD; browser/tool/code; robotics; GUI. | invalid tool calls; environment side effects; unsafe action rate; rollback count; sandbox violation rate; collision rate. | OPD learns to exploit tools/environments; recovery metric hides harmful intermediate states. | Requires instrumented environments and consistent sandbox policies. |
| Diversity and coverage | Whether distillation collapses modes or loses alternatives. | Reverse-KL OPD; diffusion/flow; instruction following; VLM. | distinct-n; self-BLEU; embedding diversity; image diversity; teacher support coverage; top-k support recall. | Mode collapse; overconfident single-style reasoning; image prompt collapse. | Diversity can trade off with correctness; needs paired capability metrics. |

## Gap Map

| gap | current state | needed evaluation method |
|---|---|---|
| Canonical exposure-bias benchmark for text OPD | No single official benchmark directly measures reduction of self-generated-state drift. | Publish a repo-level overlay protocol: sample first attempt, identify first wrong step/action with oracle or tests, condition continuation on the corrupted prefix, and report recovery/regression. |
| Teacher uncertainty transfer | Current benchmark rows mostly record task accuracy only. | Add calibration fields in experiment templates: teacher entropy bins, ECE, Brier/NLL, entropy gap, and teacher-student JS/KL on student rollouts. |
| OPD vs RLVR separation | Many reasoning and VLM results can be explained by reward-only GRPO on verifiable tasks. | Require ablations: SFT, offline KD, RLVR-only, OPD-only, OPD+RL; include IFEval/LiveBench/OOD/calibration slices. |
| Black-box/API OPD | No source-verified strict black-box OPD row; current rows are partial or borderline. | Evaluate as response-level alignment with judge robustness, not as token OPD. Require judge-swap and margin-calibration reports. |
| Privileged self-OPD leakage | Privileged context can accidentally leak answers or labels. | Add no-privilege, wrong-privilege, and delayed-privilege ablations; score calibration and recovery on originally wrong states. |
| Long-context OPD | Long-context benchmarks test retrieval and context length, not necessarily self-generated drift. | Add position-conditioned OPD diagnostics and corrupted-prefix long-context rerollout. |
| Visual teacher mismatch | Text teachers may confidently supervise visually wrong trajectories. | Add perception-heavy VLM slices and teacher-disagreement filters: OCRBench, ChartQA, DocVQA, and ambiguous visual cases. |
| Video temporal grounding | Existing table has SEED-Bench but lacks temporal grounding rows. | Add ActivityNet Captions, QVHighlights, and Charades-STA; report temporal IoU and boundary errors. |
| Speech/cross-modal OPD | Existing benchmark table has no audio/speech rows. | Add AudioBench, AIR-Bench, Dynamic-SUPERB; report ASR shortcut controls and modality-mismatch errors. |
| VLA/action OPD | Existing rows include LIBERO/RoboTwin2.0 but lack action-distribution diagnostics. | Add action KL/NLL on visited states, perturb-and-recover, horizon-conditioned success, and expert-query budget. |
| GUI grounding vs GUI agency | ScreenSpot tests grounding; OSWorld tests desktop tasks. They should not be collapsed. | Add ScreenSpot-Pro, WebArena, Mind2Web; report coordinate KL for grounding and task/recovery metrics for agents. |
| Agent tool/code OPD | Reward-only agent benchmarks are easy false positives. | Add SWE-bench Verified, AppWorld, ALFWorld/WebShop/ScienceWorld, tau-bench, BFCL with state/action teacher diagnostics. |
| Diffusion/flow OPD taxonomy | D-OPSD and Flow-OPD are borderline because velocity-field supervision is not token/logit OPD. | Evaluate with GenEval/T2I-CompBench/HEIM plus teacher-field MSE, trajectory KL proxies, diversity, and reward-overoptimization checks. |
| Private or brittle leaderboards | Hidden tests and live APIs improve contamination resistance but reduce reproducibility. | Always record split, date, evaluator version, judge model, prompt template, seed, and whether the test is private. |

## Direct Answers

- Best bundles to separate OPD from reward-only RLVR: the text reasoning plus instruction robustness bundle, OPD+RL reward-conflict bundle, VLM visual reasoning/perception bundle, and agentic tool/browser/code bundle. Each pairs reward-friendly tasks with OOD, instruction, calibration, distributional-fidelity, and recovery diagnostics.
- Benchmarks that actually test exposure bias or recovery: OSWorld, WebArena, AppWorld, SWE-bench Verified, ScienceWorld/ALFWorld/WebShop, LIBERO/RoboTwin2.0, and GUI/action tasks when evaluated with corrupted-prefix or failed-action recovery. Static MATH, GPQA, MMMU, and MathVista do not test this by themselves.
- Metrics for teacher uncertainty and fidelity: token/action KL, JS/TVD, entropy gap, top-k support recall, teacher-student rank correlation, ECE, Brier, NLL, selective risk-coverage, and entropy-conditioned accuracy.
- Contaminated/private/brittle/accuracy-only families: public static math/code/VQA benchmarks; AIME-style public exams; hidden/private leaderboards; LLM-as-judge alignment suites; live browser/desktop tasks; robotics simulators; auto-evaluated text-to-image compositional metrics; any final-answer-only leaderboard used without distributional diagnostics.
- Evaluation by method type: token/logit OPD needs direct distributional metrics; response-level black-box OPD needs judge/discriminator calibration; privileged self-OPD needs leakage and privilege ablations; action-token OPD needs visited-state action KL and recovery; diffusion/flow borderline OPD needs score/velocity-field fidelity plus diversity and image quality.
- Adjacent baselines: SFT and offline KD for strict text/VLM OPD; reward-only GRPO/PPO/RLVR for OPD+RL and VLM RLVR comparisons; DPO/IPO/SimPO/RLHF/RLAIF for preference/alignment; SPIN/SPPO/DNO/self-rewarding for self-play; behavior cloning/DAgger/online RL for VLA; WebRL/ReTool/Search-R1/SAD/T3-Agent for agents; offline diffusion distillation, consistency/LCM, DDPO, and image-preference DPO for diffusion/flow.
