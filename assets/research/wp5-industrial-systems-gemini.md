## 1. Concise Memo

**Stage-scoped strict OPD (industrial reports):**
- **Qwen3 OPD stage** (Alibaba, [arXiv 2505.09388](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.09388)): the public report exposes a strong-to-weak post-training scheme where, for lightweight Qwen3 models, "the student model first generates on-policy sequences" and the objective is "aligning its logits … to minimize the KL divergence" against larger Qwen3-32B / Qwen3-235B teacher logits. C1/C2/C3 are present but the report is terse on per-step freshness and serving mechanics. → `strict_opd` for the OPD stage; whole Qwen3 pipeline is multi-stage and must not be globally labeled OPD.
- **MiMo-V2-Flash MOPD** (Xiaomi, official tech report PDF on the [XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2-Flash GitHub](https://github.com/XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2-Flash)): §4.1 / §4.4 give an explicit MOPD surrogate with `A_MOPD,t = sg[log π_domain / π_θ]` consumed inside a PPO-style objective on student samples `y ~ μ_θ(·|x)`. → `strict_opd` scoped to the MOPD stage. Multi-teacher routing is concrete: multiple domain-specialized teachers, each scoring the current student rollout per token.
- **Nemotron-Cascade2 MOPD** (NVIDIA, [arXiv 2603.19220](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19220)): the Multi-Domain On-Policy Distillation section says "for each prompt, we sample a response" and the teacher pool is "best-performing teacher models obtained during different stages of Cascade RL training for each specific domain"; the loss is a "token-level distillation advantage using reverse KL" inside cascade RL. → `strict_opd` scoped to the MOPD substage; cascade RL stages are not globally OPD.
- **DistillSpec** (Google, [arXiv 2310.08461](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.08461), ICLR 2024): the draft-model OPD case (Equation 2 with `G` instantiated as draft generations) gives `D(M_p ‖ M_{q_θ})(y|x) = (1/|y|) Σ D(p(·|x,y_{<t}) ‖ q_θ(·|x,y_{<t}))` with the draft sampling its own `y`, and Theorem 4.1 bounds the speculative acceptance rate by the on-policy KD loss. → `strict_opd` (canonical speculative-OPD reference).

**Borderline (industrial):**
- **Gemma 2 post-training** (Google, [arXiv 2408.00118](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.00118)): §4 contains exactly one sentence — "We also run distillation from the teacher on the student's distribution" — and cites GKD/MiniLLM. C1+C2 are present but C3 is not formally specified. → `borderline_strict` for the documented OPD step. The pre-training distillation phase is offline KD, not OPD.
- **Thinking Machines Lab OPD blog** (Lu et al., Oct 27 2025, [thinkingmachines.ai/blog/on-policy-distillation](https://thinkingmachines.ai/blog/on-policy-distillation/), DOI 10.64434/tml.20251026): canonical operational definition with reference Tinker pseudocode (`do_group_rollout` + `compute_logprobs` + per-token reverse KL). Per [strict-opd-definition.md](file:///Users/bytedance/Desktop/Work/on-policy-distillation-landscape/taxonomies/strict-opd-definition.md), the blog is treated as a definitional/background source rather than a paper row. C1/C2/C3 are textbook strict for the recipe described, but the artifact is a blog and reproductions are at small scale (Qwen3-8B/32B).

**Speculative / draft-model OPD (systems lens):**
- **DistillSpec** — strict (above); the systems contribution is a *tailored f-divergence* (FKL/RKL/JSD/TVD/GJSD) chosen per task and a TVD ↔ acceptance-rate bridge (Equation 3) that justifies cheap on-policy KD as the right objective for draft alignment.
- **Speculative KD (SKD)** ([arXiv 2410.11325](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.11325)): student proposes, teacher top-K verifies, rejected tokens are *resampled from the teacher* (Algorithm 1), so the supervised prefix mixes student and teacher tokens. This is a real systems mechanism (interleaved generation) that adaptively trades off SFT ↔ OPD as training progresses, but C1 is weakened by teacher token replacement. → `partial_opd`.

**Systems-cost / serving optimizations:**
- **Lightning OPD** ([arXiv 2604.13010](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.13010)): explicitly *offline* — `D_OPD = {(q, x, {log π_T(a_t|s_t)}_t) | x ~ π_ref(·|q)}`. Teacher log-probs are **precomputed once** over `π_ref` SFT rollouts, eliminating the live teacher server; advantage `A_t(θ) = log π_T(a_t|s_t) − log π_θ(a_t|s_t)` is computed on cached data. Theorem 3.5 bounds gradient gap by `G·σ_A·√χ²(π_θ‖π_ref)`. Reports ~4× wall-clock speedup; Qwen3-8B-Base reaches 69.9% AIME 2024 in 30 GPU-hrs. C1 fails strict freshness by design. → `partial_opd`. Useful as the cleanest systems precedent for **cached log-prob** OPD.
- **Fast OPD** ([arXiv 2602.15260](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15260)): supervises only a *student-generated* prefix `L_train` that is grown on a curriculum (`L_train += ΔL=256`) because the training signal is "concentrated in the first 2000–4000 tokens" of long CoT. Objective is `E_{x~π_s} Σ_{t=1..L_train} log(π_s/π_T)`. C1 holds because the prefix is current-policy student. → `strict_opd` (over the scheduled prefix). Useful as **prefix-scoring** systems evidence.
- **REOPOLD** ([arXiv 2603.11137v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.11137v1)) and **Nemotron-Cascade2** use *sampled-token* log-prob formulations (per-token `log π_T / log π_θ`) instead of full-vocabulary teacher distributions, partially amortizing teacher cost. REOPOLD additionally entropy-masks low-information tokens to skip teacher scoring. (Reasoning audit, not industrial; included here as systems evidence.)
- **MiMo-V2-Flash MOPD** and **Nemotron-Cascade2 MOPD** require simultaneous student rollouts and per-token teacher scoring with *multiple* domain teachers. Public reports do not isolate wall-clock cost — `not found` in audited quotes.
- **Cross-tokenizer logit serving:** SimCT ([arXiv 2605.07711](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07711)) is a strict cross-tokenizer OPD primitive (minimal aligned units); KDFlow ([arXiv 2603.01875](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01875)) provides a *systems-only* contribution — FSDP2 student + SGLang teacher inference, transferring teacher hidden states (not full logits) zero-copy, recomputing logits on the student side. KDFlow is a framework, strictness depends on configuration.

**Pruning + KD as systems pipelines (false positives for strict OPD):**
- **Minitron / Mistral-NeMo-Minitron 8B** (NVIDIA) and **Llama 3.2 1B/3B** are documented offline-KD-after-pruning recipes ("incorporated logits from Llama 3.1 8B/70B to recover performance after pruning") — C1 fails (teacher-data-driven, not student rollouts). → `not_opd` / pruning-only.
- **DeepSeek-R1 Distill** ([arXiv 2501.12948](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.12948)) is SFT on R1-generated reasoning traces. C1 fails. → `not_opd`.

**Frameworks that can implement strict OPD out of the box:**
- **TRL `GKDTrainer`** ([HF docs](https://huggingface.co/docs/trl/gkd_trainer)) — text LLM only; supports the full GKD generalized JSD on student rollouts with teacher logits. → `strict_opd_capable` for the LLM case; **no official VLM-GKD example** confirmed.
- **NeMo RL** ([NVIDIA-NeMo/RL](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/RL)) — has an OPD example alongside GRPO/SFT for LLM. VLM OPD recipe `unclear`; VLM GRPO is supported.
- **Tinker / Thinking Machines cookbook** — public Python recipe (sample with `do_group_rollout`, score with `compute_logprobs`, RKL advantage) is canonical for strict OPD on Qwen3-8B/32B/235B as teacher.

**Frameworks that today only support adjacent RLVR/KD:**
- **TRL `GRPOTrainer` (VLM recipe)** — reward-only unless the user wires a custom teacher signal. → `adjacent / not strict OPD out of the box`.
- **verl** ([verl-project/verl](https://github.com/verl-project/verl)) — strong distributed PPO/GRPO/DAPO including VLM RL; strict OPD requires user-written teacher/logit dataflow. → `unclear` for OOTB strict OPD.
- **OpenRLHF** — supports KD modules and VLM RL, but no audited OOTB strict-OPD recipe. → `unclear`.

**Industrial false positives (do not promote on weak public evidence):**
- **Qwen2.5-VL / Qwen3-VL** ([2511.21631](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.21631)): public materials don't disclose teacher supervision on student on-policy multimodal trajectories. → `not_opd / unclear`.
- **InternVL3 / InternVL3.5**: SFT + MPO + Cascade RL with offline MPO and online GSPO. No teacher-on-rollout distillation signal disclosed. → `adjacent`.
- **Gemma 3** ([2503.19786](https://huggingface.co/papers/2503.19786)): "distillation" is disclosed but the public evidence does not connect it to VLM student on-policy trajectories. → `adjacent`.
- **Gemini 2.5** / **MiniCPM-V 4.5** / **LLaVA-RLHF** — RL post-training without teacher-on-rollout distillation. → `adjacent`.

**Caveats:** All 2603.x–2605.x preprints (Lightning OPD, REOPOLD, Fast OPD, Nemotron-Cascade2, MiMo-V2-Flash MOPD) post-date the assistant's knowledge cutoff. Confidence is reproduced from prior repo audits, which themselves carry a `medium` flag pending independent re-fetch.

## 5. Gap Map

| gap | what is missing | concrete next step |
|---|---|---|
| Qwen3 OPD stage exact loss form & per-step freshness | Public report does not expose the loss equation or whether the OPD leg uses the *current* student rollout at every update step. | Cross-check the official Qwen3 GitHub / release blog for the OPD-stage objective; if no per-token teacher distribution is documented at per-step freshness, downgrade to `borderline_strict`. |
| MiMo-V2-Flash MOPD serving cost | §4.4 specifies the surrogate but the report does not isolate per-token teacher-scoring cost or multi-teacher routing latency. | Read MiMo-V2-Flash paper.pdf §4.4 / §4.5 for length-control terms and teacher-batch/throughput numbers; check the GitHub release for inference-time stack and any logged training-time profiling. |
| Nemotron-Cascade2 implementation details | arXiv supports MOPD but does not report wall-clock serving cost, multi-teacher routing logic, or whether `π_inf` and `π_train` ever diverge in a way that breaks C1. | Inspect any released code/docs (NVIDIA Nemotron blog / NeMo RL recipe) for teacher routing, number of teacher forward passes per token, and rollout backend. |
| Gemma 2 post-training OPD step | One sentence + cite; no formal objective. | Track follow-up Gemma technical reports for an explicit OPD loss; current `borderline_strict` is conservative. |
| DistillSpec serving / latency | Paper proves TVD ↔ acceptance bound but does not isolate teacher-call cost during distillation training (vs. inference). | Look up DistillSpec ablations / appendix for teacher-call counts; also check whether DistillSpec recipes have been ported to `recipe/gkd/` in verl or to NeMo RL. |
| TRL GKDTrainer VLM example | No official VLM-GKD example confirmed. | Watch HF TRL release notes / recipes for a VLM-GKD module; until then, do not promote to `strict_opd_capable_VLM`. |
| NeMo RL VLM OPD recipe | NeMo RL OPD example is LLM-strong, VLM unclear. | Grep the NeMo-NeMo/RL repo for `opd` + VLM examples; if found, audit for C1/C2/C3 wiring. |
| verl / OpenRLHF strict OPD recipes | RLVR-strong; no audited OOTB strict-OPD recipe. | Open issue / PR templates; until then, `unclear_for_strict_opd` is the right OOTB label. |
| Multi-teacher routing serving evidence | MOPD-style methods (MiMo, Nemotron-Cascade2) require routing among domain teachers, but neither report exposes the routing/serving cost. | Targeted open-source-stack survey (verl, Open-R1, OpenRLHF, NeMo RL) to estimate per-token teacher-scoring overhead in mixed OPD+GRPO setups with multi-teacher routing. |
| Cross-tokenizer logit serving in industrial pipelines | KDFlow ([2603.01875](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01875)) is the cleanest systems precedent (FSDP2 + SGLang teacher hidden-state transfer) but is a framework, not a method. | Treat as systems-mechanism evidence; do not assign strictness without a configured recipe. |
| Industrial false-positives in 2025–2026 VLM/MLLM reports | InternVL3.5 online GSPO and MiniCPM-V 4.5 hybrid RL are easy to misread as OPD. | Maintain conservative `adjacent` labels until a teacher-distribution-on-rollout sentence appears in the official report. |
| Post-cutoff preprints (2603.x–2605.x) | Lightning OPD, REOPOLD, Fast OPD, Nemotron-Cascade2, MiMo-V2-Flash MOPD — all post-knowledge-cutoff. | Lead-auditor independent re-fetch (per [wp9-verifier-claude.md](file:///Users/bytedance/Desktop/Work/on-policy-distillation-landscape/assets/research/wp9-verifier/wp9-verifier-claude.md)); lift confidence from `medium` to `high` per row only after re-pull. |
