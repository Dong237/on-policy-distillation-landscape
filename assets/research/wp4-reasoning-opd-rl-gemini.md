## 1. Memo

Of 13 priority WP4 methods, **6** lock as `strict_opd` (G-OPD, REOPOLD, SOD, MiMo-V2-Flash MOPD, Nemotron-Cascade2, Fast OPD), **3** as `borderline_strict` (KDRL, SCOPE, Qwen3 OPD stage), **3** as `partial_opd` (PACED, Lightning OPD, HDPO), and **1** as `adjacent` (RLKD).

**Combine dense teacher token / log-prob supervision with GRPO/PPO/RLVR (the WP4 sweet spot):**
- **MiMo-V2-Flash (MOPD stage)** — PPO-style surrogate where the per-token advantage is a teacher–student log-ratio (`A_MOPD,t = sg[log π_domain/π_θ]`) plus an outcome reward. Strict.
- **Nemotron-Cascade2 (MOPD stage)** — token-level reverse-KL "distillation advantage" inside cascade RL with multi-teacher domain checkpoints. Strict.
- **REOPOLD** — `R_{i,t}(θ) = log π_T / π_θ` consumed as a *clipped* token reward inside a policy-gradient surrogate; strict at the loss level but the relaxed clipping weakens pure-KL semantics.
- **SOD (Step-wise OPD)** — explicit `L = L_GRPO + L_OPD_step` with step-level reverse-KL reweighting. Strict for the OPD term; full method is OPD+RL additive.
- **KDRL** — `J_KDRL = J_GRPO − β·D_KL^{k2}(π_θ‖π_T)`. KD-RKL component is strict; whole-method label is conservatively `borderline_strict` because the GRPO term remains scalar reward.
- **G-OPD** — KL-constrained reward extrapolation; reduces to OPD when scale=1 and otherwise lets the student "learn beyond teacher" by a calibrated reward-extrapolation factor.

**Reward-only RLVR despite on-policy rollouts (adjacent, not OPD):**
- **RLKD** — student rollouts via GRPO + a *learned scalar* GSRM "structural reward model" derived from teacher reasoning. Per-token teacher distribution is **not** in the loss; the teacher is collapsed to a scalar. C3 fails. → `adjacent`.

**Strict subcomponents but partial method-level recipes:**
- **PACED** — beta-kernel pass-rate curriculum applied on top of FKL **or** RKL. The FKL track uses teacher-forced rollouts (C1 fails); the RKL self-distillation track is strict. Method-level row remains `partial_opd`.
- **HDPO** — `L_HDPO = L_GRPO + λ·L_JSD`; the JSD distillation arm uses *privileged-context teacher rollouts*, not unprivileged-student rollouts (C1 fails for the distillation arm). The GRPO arm is on-policy but reward-only. Method-level `partial_opd`.
- **SCOPE** — routes student rollouts by correctness; only the *incorrect-path* branch carries dense teacher KL. Correct-path branch is student-weighted MLE (no teacher). Method-level `borderline_strict`.
- **Qwen3 OPD stage** — public Qwen3 tech report states output-logit distillation but does not expose loss equations or confirm freshness of student rollouts at every step. `borderline_strict` for the documented OPD phase.

**Long-CoT drift, mode collapse, forgetting, length inflation, teacher over-imitation:**
- **Length inflation / truncation collapse:** **Fast OPD** explicitly schedules a prefix length `L_train += ΔL` (ΔL=256) because the training signal is "concentrated in the first 2000–4000 tokens" of long CoTs. **StableOPD** (out of WP4 priority but adjacent) addresses long-CoT length inflation through a reference-divergence constraint plus rollout mixture.
- **Heavy-tailed off-policy gradients on long traces:** **REOPOLD** uses mixture-based reward clipping plus entropy-based dynamic token sampling specifically to tame heavy-tailed teacher–student log-ratios on long reasoning rollouts.
- **Teacher over-imitation:** **G-OPD** is explicitly framed as "learning beyond the teacher" via a reward-extrapolation factor λ>1; standard OPD is recovered at λ=1.
- **Teacher unreliability per step:** **SOD** introduces a per-step reliability score `d_k` that "serves as a lightweight indicator of the local reliability of teacher supervision" and reweights the OPD KL accordingly.
- **Routing failed vs. successful trajectories:** **SCOPE** routes incorrect rollouts to the teacher-KL branch and successful rollouts to a student-MLE branch, side-stepping teacher over-imitation on already-correct trajectories.
- **Mode collapse from reverse-KL OPD on reasoning** is named as a generic open failure mode in [opd-failure-modes.md](file:///Users/bytedance/Desktop/Work/on-policy-distillation-landscape/notes/opd-failure-modes.md); none of the WP4 priority methods isolate a fix in their abstracts.

**Compute / serving cost of teacher scoring during RL:**
- **Lightning OPD** is the only method in this queue that *explicitly* answers the cost question: it precomputes teacher log-probabilities once over SFT rollouts and reuses them, reporting a 4× speedup. The trade-off is that C1 fails by design (rollouts are from `π_ref`, not the current student) → `partial_opd`.
- **MiMo-V2-Flash MOPD** and **Nemotron-Cascade2** require simultaneous student-rollout sampling and per-token teacher scoring with *multiple* domain teachers; the official reports do not isolate the wall-clock cost in their abstracts.
- **REOPOLD** introduces dynamic token-level masking (entropy-based) which serves both as drift control and as a way to skip teacher scoring on low-entropy tokens, partially amortizing teacher cost.
- **KDRL, SOD, G-OPD, SCOPE, HDPO, Fast OPD, PACED, RLKD, Qwen3 OPD stage** do not surface a teacher-scoring cost discussion in the verified primary-source quotes captured in this repo.

**Caveats:**
- Source IDs in the 2603.x–2605.x range were re-fetched by subagents but flagged for independent verification per [wp9-verifier-claude.md](file:///Users/bytedance/Desktop/Work/on-policy-distillation-landscape/assets/research/wp9-verifier/wp9-verifier-claude.md). Confidence is `medium` for all such rows.
- KDRL (arXiv 2506.02208), RLKD (arXiv 2505.16142), and Qwen3 (arXiv 2505.09388) predate the cutoff and are `high` confidence.
- This memo introduces **no new arXiv IDs** beyond those already collected in WP1/WP3/WP9.
