# WP7 Candidate Line Audit - ChatGPT

Narrow scope: KEPO, D-OPSD, Flow-OPD, GTR-Turbo, plus fragile metadata checks for Video-OPD, X-OPD, Uni-OPD, PRISM, and Qwen3-VL. I used only primary arXiv papers or official reports.

## Concise Memo

Promote or update:

- KEPO: promote as stage-scoped strict OPD for the quality-gated medical VLM distillation term. The exploration branch uses privileged hints and rejection sampling, so keep the row scoped to the reward-gated OPD objective.
- Flow-OPD: promote as strict OPD for flow-matching text-to-image alignment. Unlike a generic velocity MSE claim, the paper explicitly derives a reverse-KL-equivalent dense reward over student on-policy flow states.
- Video-OPD: keep strict and update metadata: teacher is Qwen3-VL-32B-GRPO by default; Appendix A states the update is equivalent to expected reverse-KL minimization.
- X-OPD: keep strict. The source shows current-policy speech/text rollouts, a Qwen3-A3B-Instruct text teacher, token-level log-probability advantages, and a policy-gradient objective over those token signals.
- Uni-OPD: keep strict and update metadata: teacher services expose input token logprobs; margin calibration is m(q) = min positive GOPD - max negative GOPD, with target m(q) >= delta and optional shift lambda(q) = delta - m(q).

Keep below strict or out:

- D-OPSD: keep as borderline strict. It has same-rollout multimodal self-teacher velocity supervision, but the paper explicitly says the objective is not token-level KL and does not prove reverse-KL equivalence.
- GTR-Turbo: keep out of strict tables. SFT guidance uses teacher-generated reference thoughts, while KL guidance turns averaged reverse KL into an auxiliary PPO reward.
- PRISM: keep borderline strict, not strict. The MoE discriminator gives response-level scalar rewards and GRPO advantages; KL regularization is disabled.
- Qwen3-VL: no public visual-language OPD source found. The disclosed strong-to-weak OPD stage is text-only LLM-backbone distillation; multimodal RL stages use reward signals.

## Explicit Downgrade Notes

- D-OPSD should not be strict yet. It is a strong same-rollout diffusion self-distillation candidate, but its own discussion says the loss is not token-level KL and does not derive a reverse-KL-equivalent objective.
- GTR-Turbo should stay out of strict OPD tables. The SFT branch trains on teacher reference thoughts, and the KL branch inserts averaged reverse KL into PPO rewards rather than optimizing a distillation loss on the exact student state.
- PRISM should remain borderline. Its MoE discriminator is adaptive and on-policy, but the policy consumes scalar response rewards normalized into GRPO advantages; no token/logit teacher distribution is consumed by the policy loss.
- Qwen3-VL should stay out of strict multimodal OPD rows. Public sources show a Qwen3-style text-only OPD stage for the LLM backbone and reward-based multimodal RL, not visual-language OPD.
