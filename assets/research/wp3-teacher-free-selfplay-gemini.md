## Memo

WP3 is the most fragile slice of the OPD landscape because the surface vocabulary ("self-distillation", "self-play", "privileged information") matches OPD almost word-for-word, while the underlying objective is often plain SFT, plain DPO, or plain RLVR with no token-level distillation signal. Of the 11 priority methods plus 5 self-play preference variants audited here:

- **3 lock as `strict_opd`** when the privileged-self-teacher reading of C2 is accepted: **SDPO** (arXiv 2601.20802), **OPCD** (arXiv 2602.12275), and the **OPSD** substage of Privileged Information Distillation (arXiv 2602.04942). All three explicitly sample y from the current student π_θ and supervise those exact tokens with a same-weights-but-privileged or feedback-conditioned self-teacher distribution under reverse KL.
- **2 are `borderline_strict`**: **OPSDC / CRISP** (arXiv 2603.05433) — strict in the abstract, but the teacher snapshot is synced every K steps so freshness is "fresh-K-stale" rather than fully online; and **OEL** (arXiv 2603.16856) — strict only for the OPCD-style consolidation substage of the deployment loop.
- **1 is `partial_opd`**: **HDPO** (arXiv 2603.23871) — the distillation arm uses *privileged-info-conditioned rollouts* (teacher-side) rather than the unconditioned student rollout; only the RL arm operates on student rollouts but is reward-only.
- **3 are `not_opd`**: **GATES** (arXiv 2602.20574, tutor-side rollouts); **π-Distill joint objective** (arXiv 2602.04942, teacher-side rollouts; the OPSD subroutine is the strict companion); **SDFT** (arXiv 2402.13669, one-shot self-rewriting → SFT cross-entropy with no distributional teacher signal).
- **1 is `adjacent`**: **SPIN** (arXiv 2401.01335) — current iterate vs. previous-iterate self-play, but the objective is a DPO-style log-ratio loss with the SFT target as winner; no teacher distribution is consumed token-wise. Same downgrade applies to all checked self-play preference variants (**SPPO**, **DNO**, **Self-Rewarding LM**, **IPO** under self-play wrappers, **SimPO** under self-play wrappers).

**Why so many "self-distillation" methods are not OPD.** The key C3 failure is using the previous-checkpoint or self-play opponent only to *generate negatives* for a preference loss (DPO/IPO/SPO/SimPO log-ratio), or only to *rewrite a dataset* and then run plain SFT on the rewrite. In neither case does the objective consume a token-level teacher distribution on the current student's rollout. The C1 failure mode is using the privileged-context self-teacher to *generate the rollout* (so the supervised tokens are the privileged side's, not the unconditioned student's) — this is the GATES / π-Distill / HDPO failure. OPSD, OPCD, SDPO are precisely the strict companions where the privileged or feedback-conditioned self-teacher only *grades* tokens that the unconditioned current student emitted.

**Caveats.** Many entries are 2602.x–2605.x preprints post-cutoff; quotes here are abstract/HTML-level unless an Algorithm-1 line was cited in [wp1-line-audit-chatgpt.md](file:///Users/bytedance/Desktop/Work/on-policy-distillation-landscape/assets/research/wp1-line-audit-chatgpt.md) or the gemini line audit. Where the equation was not exposed in the abstract, confidence is downgraded rather than inferred. The "self-play DPO/SPO/IPO" rows are conservatively labelled `adjacent` because their primary sources do not present a token-level distillation objective.

## Gap map

The WP3 slice has the following structural blind spots that primary-source evidence in this round did not resolve:

1. **OPSDC/CRISP teacher-snapshot freshness.** The primary source describes "iterative self-policy distillation" with the concise-instruction-conditioned teacher synced every K steps. The exact K and the regret incurred by stale-teacher steps are not exposed in the abstract. Strict label requires a §-level read of the snapshot-sync schedule. Currently labelled `borderline_strict`.
2. **OEL deployment-stage scoping.** OEL is a multi-stage loop — only the consolidation substage uses OPCD-style on-policy distillation. The deployment-collection and knowledge-extraction substages are not OPD. The repository should track substage-level labels rather than paper-level labels for OEL. Currently labelled `borderline_strict` with an explicit substage scope.
3. **HDPO arm isolation.** The distillation arm of HDPO uses privileged-info-conditioned rollouts (failed C1) while the RL arm uses student rollouts (failed C3). Neither arm alone is strict; the combination does not become strict by addition. Open question: is there a configuration of HDPO in the codebase where the privileged teacher supervises *student-generated* tokens (i.e., an OPSD-style C2 reading of HDPO)? Primary-source abstract does not state this; needs code/§-level audit.
4. **Self-play preference variants vs. self-play distillation variants.** SPIN/SPPO/DNO/Self-Rewarding/IPO/SimPO are all preference-loss based and adjacent. The literature does not yet provide a clean self-play *distillation* analogue where the previous iterate supplies a *token distribution* (rather than scalar preferences) on the current iterate's rollouts. Open question for WP3: is anyone running EMA-anchor or previous-checkpoint **token-level KL** on student rollouts? Such a method would qualify as `strict_opd` under the previous-checkpoint reading of C2; none was found in this priority queue. (BOND uses a Best-of-N implied distribution against a reward model — adjacent, not a teacher distribution.)
5. **SDFT vs. "SDFT-Continual" name collision.** "SDFT" in 2024 (Yang et al., 2402.13669) is one-shot self-rewritten SFT and is `not_opd`. A later 2026 "SDFT-Continual" reportedly performs more OPD-like updates; this audit did **not** verify that paper from a primary source. Mark unresolved; needs explicit acronym disambiguation before any promotion.
6. **GATES tutor reading.** The abstract is unambiguous that the tutor (privileged-document-conditioned same model) samples the supervised traces. Open question: does the GATES codebase contain a configuration where the *student* samples and the tutor only grades? If so, that configuration would be `borderline_strict` (privileged-context teacher reading of C2). Primary-source abstract does not surface that variant.
7. **Privileged-self-teacher rule under the strict-OPD definition.** The current strict-OPD definition explicitly accepts a same-weight model as teacher when it has privileged context, answers, memory, or demonstrations the student does not see. SDPO/OPCD/OPSD are the canonical exemplars. No new rule changes are required from WP3, but the OPSD/π-Distill split confirms that **paper-level labels are unsafe for this slice — substage-level labels are required**.
8. **Verifier-as-teacher overlap with WP9.** Some self-play methods use a verifier (rule-based or learned) as the C2 supervisor. This audit deliberately does not absorb verifier-only methods (those belong to WP9). The boundary is: if the verifier emits a *scalar* score → `adjacent`; if the verifier emits a *token-level distribution or per-token signal that the loss consumes distributionally* → potentially `strict_opd` under WP9 rules. None of the SPIN/SPPO/DNO/Self-Rewarding/IPO/SimPO family crosses that boundary.
