# WP0: Survey Replication and Citation Audit

**Starting survey:** Mingyang Song and Mao Zheng, "A Survey of On-Policy Distillation for Large Language Models," arXiv: [2604.00626](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00626), submitted 2026-04-01.

**Audit date:** 2026-05-11

**Audit rule:** A method is `strict_opd` only if the current/student policy generates training trajectories, a teacher/expert/discriminator/reference/privileged-context source supervises those trajectories, and the objective uses that supervision. Reward-only RLVR/GRPO/RLHF, offline KD, static teacher traces, static DPO, and ordinary synthetic-data SFT are not strict OPD.

## A. Research Memo

The survey's core thesis is sound: off-policy LLM distillation trains students on fixed teacher/corpus prefixes, while inference exposes the student to its own generated prefixes. This train-test mismatch is the autoregressive version of exposure bias. OPD exists to query a supervisor on states the student actually visits, borrowing the DAgger/interactive-imitation argument that expert feedback on learner states reduces compounding error. In the survey's mathematical framing, OPD samples `y` from a behavior policy with nonzero student/current-policy mass and minimizes an `f`-divergence or related feedback loss between teacher and student along those sampled prefixes.

The survey's three-axis taxonomy is useful, but it over-broadens the OPD boundary. The feedback axis mixes strict dense teacher supervision with scalar outcome rewards and DPO-like preference optimization. Under this audit's strict definition, reward-only RLVR is adjacent even if it is on-policy, because no teacher/distillation supervision is applied to the student trajectory. Similarly, some cited "logit" methods are OPD-compatible losses rather than independently OPD methods unless the paper actually uses student-generated prefixes.

The best-supported strict OPD core is: GKD, MiniLLM, DistiLLM, Entropy-Aware OPD, OPSD, GAD/black-box OPD in a borderline response-level regime, and industrial MOPD-style systems such as MiMo-V2-Flash and Nemotron-Cascade 2. The primary GKD source explicitly trains on self-generated outputs with teacher feedback. MiniLLM explicitly derives an on-policy reverse-KL objective. DistiLLM uses student-generated outputs plus an adaptive replay/off-policy efficiency component. Entropy-Aware OPD explicitly describes dense token-level signals along the student's own trajectories. OPSD, per the survey's quoted formulation, uses student rollouts and a privileged self-teacher conditioned on the answer.

The main audit corrections are:

- **Do not elevate OPD-compatible losses to strict OPD without rollout evidence.** ToDi, DSKD, AKL/AdaKD/SelecTKD, TAID, PromptKD, ULD, and related distribution/loss papers may be valuable OPD components, but the primary source must show student-generated trajectories.
- **Downgrade mixed-policy recipes.** DistiLLM-2, AdaSwitch, ORPO-Distill, LUFFY, AlignDistil, DASD, and SuperCorrect mix student rollouts with teacher-generated/off-policy traces, preference pairs, or RL rewards. Track the OPD component, not the whole method, as strict.
- **Keep DeepSeek-R1 distillation out of strict OPD.** The survey itself states that DeepSeek-R1 distillation uses about 800K static teacher samples and SFT, with no student rollouts or logit matching.
- **Separate OPD from RLVR.** GRPO/RLVR is on-policy RL, but it becomes strict OPD only when the objective also uses teacher/expert/discriminator/privileged-context supervision on the student rollouts.
- **Industrial claims need sharper sourcing.** Qwen3, MiMo-V2-Flash, and Nemotron-Cascade 2 have primary evidence for OPD/MOPD. Gemma 2's "online KD" claim is not enough, by itself, to prove strict OPD during pre-training.

Bottom line: the survey is a strong organizing map, but the repository should distinguish (1) strict OPD loops, (2) OPD-compatible objectives and engineering tricks, (3) mixed-policy KD+RL recipes, and (4) adjacent reward-only or offline distillation.

## B. Taxonomy Reconstruction

### Feedback Signal

| category | audited meaning | representative strict/core examples | boundary notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| logit-based | Teacher or privileged model logits/log-probs supervise student-generated prefixes. | GKD, MiniLLM, DistiLLM, Entropy-Aware OPD, REOPOLD, Fast OPD, Veto, TSD-KD, OPSD | ToDi/DSKD/AdaKD/SelecTKD are OPD-compatible unless their own experiment samples student trajectories. |
| outcome/reward-guided | Student rollouts receive scalar, verbal, verifier, reward-model, or preference feedback. | OVD, GAD, SCoRe, some KDRL/RLKD variants | Reward-only GRPO/RLVR is adjacent, not strict OPD. |
| self-play/teacher-free | The feedback source is a prior checkpoint, human/reference distribution, or privileged-context variant of the same model. | OPSD, OPSDC, GATES, Privileged Information Distillation | SPIN is borderline because its positive anchor is human data and negatives are previous-policy outputs. |
| hybrid KD+RL | Student rollouts receive both distillation-style supervision and rewards. | VOLD, MiMo MOPD, Nemotron MOPD, some Qwen3 post-training | If the KD term is only static/off-policy, classify the overall recipe as partial. |

### Teacher Access

| category | audited meaning | examples | boundary notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| white-box | Teacher logits/log-probs or hidden states are available on student prefixes. | GKD, MiniLLM, DistiLLM, Entropy-Aware, REOPOLD, Fast OPD | This is the cleanest strict OPD setting. |
| black-box/API | Teacher gives text, ratings, rankings, or discriminator data, not full logits. | GAD, Lion, OVD, ORPO-Distill, SuperCorrect | Often `borderline_strict` or `partial_opd` because supervision is sequence-level or preference-like. |
| teacher-free/self | Feedback comes from same model under privileged context, previous checkpoint, or reference data. | OPSD, GATES, SPIN, SDPO, HDPO, TMS | Strictness depends on whether current student rollouts are supervised. |
| multi-teacher | Multiple teachers or domain teachers route dense supervision. | MiMo-V2-Flash, Nemotron-Cascade 2, MAD-OPD | Should be promoted to an explicit practical axis. |

### Loss Granularity

| category | audited meaning | examples | notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| token-level | Per-token divergence/supervision along student prefixes. | GKD, DistiLLM, Entropy-Aware, OPSD, Fast OPD, MiMo/Nemotron MOPD | Low variance, high teacher-query cost. |
| sequence-level | Complete trajectory reward, likelihood ratio, discriminator, or teacher sequence score. | MiniLLM, KETCHUP, Constrained CMDP, GAD, Lion | Often high variance or response-level only. |
| hybrid/adaptive | Combines token and sequence, or changes weighting by token/difficulty/stage. | DistiLLM-2, PACED, AdaSwitch, VOLD, KDRL, RLKD | Must separate the strict OPD term from RL/off-policy terms. |

Recommended repository addition: keep the survey's three axes but add **rollout source** as a fourth audit axis: `student_on_policy`, `mixed_policy`, `teacher_generated`, `static_dataset`, `environment_rollout`, `unclear`.
