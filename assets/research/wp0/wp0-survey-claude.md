# WP0: Replication and Citation Audit of the Tencent OPD Survey

**Survey audited:** Mingyang Song & Mao Zheng, *A Survey of On-Policy Distillation for Large Language Models*, arXiv:2604.00626 (Tencent, 1 Apr 2026).
**Audit date:** 11 May 2026.
**Strict OPD criterion used throughout:** (C1) the student/current policy generates training trajectories; (C2) teacher/expert/discriminator/reference/privileged-context supervision is applied to those student-generated trajectories; (C3) the training objective consumes that supervision. Reward-only RLVR/GRPO/RLHF, offline KD, SFT on teacher traces, static DPO, synthetic-data SFT, and untethered self-training are NOT strict OPD.

---

## A. Research memo

**Core thesis.** The survey argues that on-policy distillation (OPD) is the principled fix for *exposure bias* in autoregressive LLM distillation. Conventional KD is off-policy: students train under teacher-forcing on fixed teacher- or human-generated prefixes, yet at inference must condition on their own (potentially erroneous) tokens. The induced covariate shift between the dataset state-visitation distribution `d_𝒟(s)` and the student's own induced distribution `d_{π_θ}(s)` causes O(εT²) compounding error under behavior cloning. By having the student sample trajectories from `p_θ` and soliciting teacher feedback on those self-generated states (DAgger reduction, Ross et al. 2011), error shrinks to O(εT). The survey's central technical contribution is a *unified f-divergence-over-on-policy-samples* objective `𝔼_{y∼π_mix}[Σ_t 𝒟_f(p_T,p_θ)]` that subsumes GKD, MiniLLM, DistiLLM and successors as different choices of (i) the mixing policy `π_mix`, (ii) the divergence generator `f`, and (iii) argument order/target.

**Key audit findings.** (1) The survey's foundational citations are accurate and the primary-source classifications check out: GKD at λ=1 (Agarwal et al., ICLR 2024), MiniLLM (Gu et al., ICLR 2024), GAD (Ye et al., 2025), DistillSpec (Zhou et al., ICLR 2024) are unambiguous **strict_opd**. (2) The survey is **honest about borderline cases**: DistiLLM's authors themselves describe their student-generated outputs as "adaptive off-policy" replay (per-epoch batched, not per-iteration), so the canonical reference often cited as "the" OPD method is in fact borderline by the strict definition. DistiLLM-2 is the same. (3) The survey **correctly classifies DeepSeek-R1 distilled checkpoints as off-policy** (§6.3), which is the single most important false-positive trap in the broader community; primary source (arXiv 2501.12948) confirms "we apply only SFT and do not include an RL stage". (4) Industrial OPD claims for **Qwen3, MiMo-V2-Flash MOPD, Nemotron-Cascade 2, VOLD, Video-OPD and X-OPD all check out** as genuine strict OPD per primary sources. (5) The survey's characterization of **Gemma 2 as "Online KD in pre-training" is misleading** — the Gemma 2 paper describes pre-training KD as standard Hinton soft-target replacement of next-token prediction over a fixed corpus; the true on-policy step is in post-training only (citing Agarwal 2023). (6) ToDi, AKL, TAID, PromptKD, ATKD, AlignDistil, f-DISTILL, SeqKD, TED are all off-policy in their original formulations; the survey acknowledges this for ToDi/AKL but groups them under OPD because their adaptive-weighting mechanisms transfer directly to on-policy sampling — a defensible-but-liberal inclusion. (7) SPIN is included as "teacher-free self-play" but under the strictest reading violates condition (C2): there is no external teacher distribution, only a logistic loss over older self-versus-SFT-data pairs (DPO-equivalent). The survey's self-play/teacher-free axis stretches the OPD definition to encompass this.

**Audit conclusions.** The survey is a **competent, current, and analytically rigorous** consolidation of a genuinely fragmented field. Its central unification framework is sound. Its method classifications track primary sources within ~10% margin of error on strictness. The principal weaknesses are (i) liberal inclusion of off-policy adaptive-weighting work (ToDi, AKL, TAID, PromptKD) as "OPD-relevant" without consistent strictness flagging; (ii) a misleading "online KD" label for Gemma 2's pre-training stage; (iii) several quantitative industrial claims (MiMo-V2 "<1/50 compute", Qwen3 "OPD beats RL") are corporate-marketing-style numbers whose precise experimental controls are not fully isolated in the primary tech reports; (iv) one canonical omission — Thinking Machines Lab's October 2025 "On-Policy Distillation" blog post (Lu et al.), which popularized the term in the community and which the Qwen3 OPD pipeline cites. Several user-prompted method names — **SCOPE-as-"Self-play Contrastive On-Policy Evaluation"** and **DistillDirect** — do not correspond to any real OPD paper; the actual SCOPE in the survey is *Signal-Calibrated On-Policy Distillation Enhancement* (Zheng et al., Meituan, arXiv 2604.10688) which is strict OPD.

## B. Taxonomy reconstruction and assessment

The survey organizes OPD along three orthogonal axes:

| Axis | Categories | Representative methods (per survey) |
|---|---|---|
| **Feedback signal** | Logit-based / Outcome-based (reward) / Self-play | GKD, MiniLLM, DistiLLM, ToDi, Entropy-Aware OPD (logit) · RLKD, KDRL, LUFFY, X-KD (outcome) · SPIN, OPSD, GATES, SDPO (self-play) |
| **Teacher access** | White-box / Black-box / Self-distillation (teacher-free or privileged-context self-teacher) | DistiLLM, GKD, MiniLLM (white-box) · Lion, GAD (black-box) · SPIN, OPSD, OPSDC, GATES, SDPO, Priv-Info-Distill, HDPO, OPCD, OEL, SDFT, TMS, MTP-Self-Distill (self-distillation) |
| **Loss granularity** | Token-level / Sequence-level / Hybrid-adaptive | GKD, DistiLLM, ToDi, AKL (token) · MiniLLM, Constrained-CMDP, KETCHUP (sequence) · SuperCorrect, PACED, AdaSwitch, X-KD, Fast OPD (hybrid) |

**Assessment of completeness.** The three axes are *genuinely orthogonal and exhaustive in the design space they cover.* The taxonomy correctly anticipates underexplored combinations (e.g., black-box × token-level, currently sparse — only GAD and ORPO-Distill populate this corner). Strengths: (1) the f-divergence unification cleanly maps GKD↔MiniLLM↔DistiLLM as parameterizations of one objective; (2) the privileged-context-as-self-teacher template (OPSD/GATES/SDFT/Priv-Info/HDPO/OPCD/OEL all share this structure) is well-identified; (3) the granularity axis correctly distinguishes PRMs / step-level supervision as a hybrid category. Gaps in the taxonomy: (a) **no axis for trajectory freshness** (per-iteration vs per-epoch vs replay-buffer-stale rollouts), which is the critical strictness dimension separating GKD from DistiLLM; (b) **no axis for teacher kind** (separate-larger-LLM vs same-model-privileged-context vs older-self-checkpoint vs reward-model-induced-target), which the audit reveals to be central; (c) the "outcome-based" cell collapses RLVR-with-teacher-reward (KDRL, RLKD) with pure-RLVR (which is *not* OPD) — a finer split is needed to avoid the very false-positive trap (GRPO≠OPD) the survey otherwise navigates correctly.

## F. Gap map — needed deeper research

**High-priority omissions (likely should be in the survey):**
1. **Thinking Machines Lab "On-Policy Distillation"** (Kevin Lu et al., Oct 26 2025, thinkingmachines.ai/blog/on-policy-distillation, DOI 10.64434/tml.20251026). The single most community-influential 2025 OPD write-up; not cited in the survey body I retrieved. Likely missed because it is a non-arXiv blog. **Strict_opd** canonical exposition.
2. **ReST-EM / "Beyond Human Data"** (Singh et al. DeepMind, arXiv:2312.06585). The canonical expert-iteration paper. The survey discusses expert iteration conceptually without citing this foundational work. Adjacent / not_opd but topically necessary.
3. **AMPO: Adaptive Multi-Guidance Policy Optimization** (Yuan et al., Oct 2025, arXiv:2510.02227). Multi-teacher trace insertion into RLVR; borderline OPD. Directly relevant to the "Black-box / multi-teacher" cell.
4. **Self-Rewarding LMs / Meta-Rewarding LMs** (Yuan et al. Meta 2024, arXiv:2401.10020 / 2407.19594). The survey covers SPIN as teacher-free but omits this closely adjacent Meta line.
5. **Phi-4-reasoning** (arXiv:2504.21318). Would be a useful off-policy contrast point in §6.3 alongside DeepSeek-R1.

**Definitional / classification work needed:**
6. **Trajectory-freshness taxonomy axis.** Distinguish (i) per-iteration on-policy (GKD λ=1, MiniLLM, Veto, OPSD, OPCD), (ii) per-epoch batched on-policy (DistiLLM-2), (iii) replay-buffer-stale on-policy (DistiLLM), (iv) own-historical-checkpoint (TMS), (v) precomputed-on-SFT-rollouts (Lightning OPD). This dimension is the key strictness discriminator and is missing from the survey's three axes.
7. **Disaggregate "outcome-based" feedback.** Survey lumps RLKD (teacher-derived structural reward) with pure RLVR (no teacher at all). Cleaner split: teacher-derived-reward vs. teacher-independent-verifier-reward.
8. **Teacher-kind axis.** Separate-larger-LLM vs same-model-privileged-context vs older-self-checkpoint vs RM-induced-target. The privileged-context-as-self-teacher cluster (OPSD/GATES/SDFT/Priv-Info/HDPO/OPCD/OEL/OPSDC) is a coherent template deserving its own column.

**Industrial claims requiring further substantiation:**
9. **MiMo-V2-Flash's "<1/50 compute of SFT+RL"** claim originates from Xiaomi marketing pages, not the arXiv 2601.02780 ablation tables. Needs controlled-benchmark verification.
10. **Qwen3's "distillation outperforms RL in performance and training efficiency"** is in the abstract; the precise compute-cost numbers for the OPD-only stage are not isolated in the tech report. WP1 should attempt to derive or bound these numbers from the released training logs (if any).
11. **Gemma 2 "Online KD in pre-training"** survey label needs correction or qualification. Primary source describes Hinton-style offline KD in pre-training; the on-policy stage exists only in post-training.

**Unverified / non-existent named methods:**
12. **DistillDirect** — no matching paper located in LLM OPD literature; recommend removing from canonical method lists.
13. **SCOPE as "Self-play Contrastive On-Policy Evaluation"** — incorrect gloss; the actual SCOPE (Signal-Calibrated OPD, arxiv.org/abs/2604.10688) is real and strict OPD but unrelated to the gloss.
14. **DBKD** — no canonical OPD paper; likely a generic category label.
15. **TED** as OPD — the named "TED" (Liang et al., ICML'23, arXiv:2210.01351) is encoder-LM layer-wise KD, NOT OPD. Any future reference to "TED" as OPD is suspect.

**Strictness-revision candidates within the survey:**
16. Re-classify **DistiLLM / DistiLLM-2** from implied strict_opd → **borderline** (authors explicitly call SGO usage "adaptive off-policy" / "batched not on-policy").
17. Re-classify **SPIN** and other teacher-free self-play under a clearly-flagged sub-category to avoid implying external-teacher supervision.
18. Re-classify **Lion** explicitly as adversarial *curriculum* (adjacent) rather than as black-box OPD, since the loss is supervised CE on teacher completions, not on student trajectories.
19. Flag **LUFFY** as RL-with-off-policy-guidance rather than as OPD; teacher logits are not used.
20. Flag **AlignDistil / TAID / ToDi / AKL / PromptKD** consistently as **off-policy adaptive techniques transferable to OPD**, not as OPD methods themselves.
