# WP7 Multimodal Frontier Audit

Agent: ChatGPT
Date: 2026-05-13
Scope: VLM, MLLM, video, speech, VLA, GUI, and multimodal-agent methods. WP6 agentic candidates were not reopened except GUI-SD and LiteGUI.

## 1. Concise Memo

Strict OPD: VOLD, Video-OPD, X-OPD, Uni-OPD, VLA-OPD, GUI-SD, and the GKD stage of LiteGUI satisfy C1/C2/C3. Each has current student rollouts or trajectories, teacher or privileged-context supervision on those same states, and a token/logit/action-token/coordinate-token distillation objective rather than only scalar reward.

Borderline: PRISM has current-policy rollouts and an adaptive multimodal discriminator, but the consumed signal is response-level MoE reward/advantages under GRPO. I do not classify it as strict under this audit rule.

Partial or stage-scoped: LiteGUI is strict only for its GKD stage; its later GRPO stage is reward-only. Qwen3-VL discloses on-policy distillation with KL, but the report says this strong-to-weak distillation uses text-only data to fine-tune the LLM backbone, so it is not strict multimodal OPD evidence.

Adjacent: VLM-R1, Perception-R1, R1-VL/StepGRPO, Vision-R1, DeepVideo-R1, VTool-R1, OpenVLThinker, InternVL3.5, and MiniCPM-V use RLVR, GRPO variants, judges, rewards, DPO, or SFT/RL cycles without same-rollout distributional teacher supervision.

Held-out / not OPD: LLaVA-KD, LLAVADI, Visual Program Distillation, Gemma 3, and Gemini are offline/static distillation or program/data distillation sources. They remain useful related work but fail C1 for strict OPD.

No new methods are recommended here beyond the requested set. Several external frontier candidates may merit a later WP9 line audit, but they are not added without primary-source verification in this pass.
