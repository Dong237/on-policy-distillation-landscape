## Memo

None of the 5 WP1 redirect candidates clear strict OPD under the conservative reading. **ADPA** is the closest — it uses a distribution-level teacher advantage over student-generated states in its strongest configuration (Appendix E ablation) — so it is labeled `borderline_strict` with an explicit caveat that the main experiments use preference-data states (y_w, y_l) rather than fresh rollouts, and the objective is advantage-guided (policy-gradient-flavored) rather than a dense teacher KL.

**PAD** has student-sampled responses and a teacher-distribution-style preference loss (JSD over Plackett-Luce distributions built from teacher log-probability rewards on student responses), but the supervision granularity is a response-set preference distribution rather than dense per-state teacher probabilities, and the training loop does not re-sample the student at every gradient step. Labeled `partial_opd`.

**GAKD** fails C1 by design: the paper and author rebuttal explicitly state that reverse-KL is optimized on **teacher-generated sequences** via importance sampling, and that on-policy student sampling is the drawback the method avoids. The discriminator supplies a sequence-level adversarial signal, but student training inputs are not student rollouts. Labeled `partial_opd`.

**daDPO** and **CTPD** are DPO-family preference-distillation methods over static preference pairs. daDPO samples y^s from π_st once to construct the preference dataset, then runs DPO with a sentence-level KL to the teacher distribution; there is no per-step student rollout refresh. CTPD operates on standard (x, y_w, y_l) preference data with no student rollout loop, using white-box teacher log-probs projected across tokenizers as a DPO-style reference. Both are labeled `partial_opd`.

**Caveats:**
- All quotes are taken from abstracts, HTML v1/v2, OpenReview forum pages, or the ACL Anthology PDF. I did not have access to full PDFs for line-by-line code verification.
- GAKD is an active ICLR 2026 submission; the clearest statement that student samples are not used comes from the authors' own reviewer rebuttal (OpenReview comment) rather than the paper body.
- ADPA's Appendix E is referenced in the abstract-level TOC (`Impact of the Sampling Sources of State s_t`), but I could not retrieve the exact quote from that appendix section; the borderline label relies on the main-text claim that ADPA delivers distribution-level advantage and the known structure of the objective over preference-pair states.
