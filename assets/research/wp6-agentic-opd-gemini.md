## 1. Concise Memo

WP6 ("agentic and interactive OPD") is the smallest strict-OPD slice of the landscape and the largest false-positive cluster in the entire repo. The agentic vocabulary — "online agent learning", "tool-use distillation", "self-correction", "experience learning", "browser RL" — overlaps almost word-for-word with strict OPD, while the underlying training signal is overwhelmingly scalar outcome reward, reward-model output, or static SFT on synthetic tool traces. Of the priority list audited here, **5** lock as `strict_opd` (MAD-OPD, SOD, OPCD, VLA-OPD, GUI-SD), **1** as `partial_opd` at the method level with a strict consolidation substage (OEL), and the remaining priority-and-sweep agentic systems are `adjacent` reward-only RL (SCoRe, WebRL, ReTool, VTool-R1, Search-R1, Agent-R1), `not_opd` verbal-only frameworks (Reflexion), `not_opd` offline imitation (T3-Agent / MM-Traj, Visual Program Distillation), or evaluation/system papers (SWE-agent).

**Strict agentic OPD (C1+C2+C3 against verified primary quotes):**
- **MAD-OPD / OPAD** (arXiv 2605.01347) — multi-agent debate teachers force-decode the student's on-policy action / token at every step of the agentic trajectory; loss is a confidence-weighted JSD/RKL mathematically equivalent to "dense token-level RL in which the divergence D directly shapes the reward signal" ([wp1-line-audit-gemini.md](file:///Users/bytedance/Desktop/Work/on-policy-distillation-landscape/assets/research/wp1-line-audit-gemini.md#L35)). The **OPAD substage** that adds per-step environment observations is the most WP6-relevant strict row: it is the cleanest example in the landscape of an agent-token teacher KL applied to a current-policy multi-step trajectory.
- **SOD** (arXiv 2605.07725) — explicit `L = L_GRPO + L_OPD_step` with a step-level reliability gate `d_k` over student-generated tool-integrated reasoning trajectories. SOD is the only WP6 row that explicitly addresses **cascading tool-induced state drift** by reweighting the OPD KL per step, with tool-observation tokens excluded from the KL ([wp4-reasoning-opd-rl-gemini.md](file:///Users/bytedance/Desktop/Work/on-policy-distillation-landscape/assets/research/wp4-reasoning-opd-rl-gemini.md#L67)).
- **OPCD** (arXiv 2602.12275) — privileged-context self-teacher consumes per-token reverse KL on the student's own (no-context) rollout; this is the canonical primitive that every other privileged-context agentic recipe reuses (OEL consolidation, GUI-SD, OPSD/π-Distill).
- **VLA-OPD** (arXiv 2603.26666) — robotic action-token OPD: a frozen expert VLA π_tea is queried on the student's self-generated states (Algorithm 1 line 12) and a per-token negative-RKL intrinsic reward `r_t = −(log π_θ − log π_tea)` is plugged into a per-token PG update (Algorithm 1 line 20). This is the only physical-environment / robotics row with verified C1+C2+C3 ([wp9-line-audit-vla-prism-gemini.md](file:///Users/bytedance/Desktop/Work/on-policy-distillation-landscape/assets/research/wp9-line-audit-vla-prism-gemini.md#L31)).
- **GUI-SD** (arXiv 2605.00642) — privileged-visual self-teacher (target bounding box / soft mask / hint) supplies a weighted reverse-KL on the student's coordinate-output tokens, especially the higher-order digits. Strict for GUI **grounding**, not for multi-step GUI control. (Co-audit row sourced from [wp6-agentic-opd-chatgpt.md](file:///Users/bytedance/Desktop/Work/on-policy-distillation-landscape/assets/research/wp6-agentic-opd-chatgpt.md); flagged for independent re-fetch.)

**Partial OPD (substage strict, full pipeline mixed):**
- **OEL** (arXiv 2603.16856) — strict only for the **OPCD-style consolidation substage** ("knowledge-conditioned output of a teacher … on-policy context distillation … token-level reverse KL divergence", per [wp1-line-audit-gemini.md](file:///Users/bytedance/Desktop/Work/on-policy-distillation-landscape/assets/research/wp1-line-audit-gemini.md#L44)). The full OEL loop also includes deployment-trajectory collection (off-policy w.r.t. server-side training) and an experience-extraction pass; the method-level row stays `partial_opd`.

**Reward-only RLVR for agents (adjacent, NOT strict OPD):**
- **SCoRe** (arXiv 2409.12917) — multi-turn online RL on self-generated correction traces with a reward bonus and a KL regularizer to the **base policy** (not a teacher); no teacher distribution is consumed token-wise. C1 holds, C2 fails (no teacher), C3 fails (scalar reward + base-KL). → `adjacent`. This is the canonical false-positive anchor for "multi-turn online" claims that look like OPD.
- **WebRL, ReTool, VTool-R1, Search-R1, Agent-R1** — all use student-generated multi-turn web/code/search/tool trajectories but consume only outcome reward, ORM scores, or task-success scalar reward. No teacher logits, no token/action-level KL. → `adjacent`.

**Verbal RL with no weight update (not OPD at all):**
- **Reflexion** (arXiv 2303.11366) — verbal feedback stored in episodic memory; the abstract is explicit that the framework operates "without updating model weights". No training objective, no C3. → `not_opd / no-training`.

**Offline tool-imitation / synthetic-tool-trace SFT (not OPD):**
- **T3-Agent / MM-Traj** (arXiv 2412.15606) — VLM is SFT-tuned on GPT-4o-mini-generated tool trajectories. C1 fails (trained student does not generate the supervised trajectory).
- **Visual Program Distillation** (arXiv 2312.03052) — LLM samples programs, verifies, translates, and distills into a VLM via offline KD on the verified programs. C1 fails (static verified programs, not student-visited states).
- **SWE-agent** (arXiv 2405.15793) — agent-computer interface paper; not a training/distillation method. → `system_not_training`.

**Tool-call correction methods (mostly absent at strict tier):** the WP6 prompt explicitly looks for "tool-call correction methods" and "earliest-error" supervision. SOD's step-reliability gate is the closest verified strict row. No paper in this audit was found to apply explicit **action-level teacher demonstrations or labels at the earliest-error tool call** on a current-policy trajectory and consume them via a token/action KL; this is a real WP6 gap (see §5).

**Browser / GUI agent training (mostly absent at strict tier):** WebRL and GUI-only RL papers are reward-only; only GUI-SD (single-step grounding under privileged visual context) qualifies as strict OPD. **Long-horizon browser strict OPD is not yet in evidence in any audited primary source.**

**Multi-turn web / search agents:** Search-R1 is the canonical adjacent example. No multi-turn search-agent strict-OPD method was identified.

**Safety constraints during on-policy exploration:** essentially none of the WP6 strict rows expose explicit safety protocols. VLA-OPD relies on simulator/benchmark settings, GUI-SD on static GUI grounding datasets, SOD on tool-integrated math/code sandboxes, OPCD/OEL on text-only prompts, MAD-OPD on inherited refusal behavior. The landscape is essentially silent on **safety-constrained online exploration in deployed environments**; this is a gap rather than a finding.

**Systems cost summary (verified):** strict agentic OPD replaces sparse delayed reward with dense teacher calls and pays simultaneously for student rollouts, environment/tool execution, and per-step teacher (or debate ensemble) forward passes. GUI-SD reports ≈4.2 GPU-hr/epoch vs. ≈16.7 hr/epoch for GRPO baselines (see [wp6-agentic-opd-chatgpt.md](file:///Users/bytedance/Desktop/Work/on-policy-distillation-landscape/assets/research/wp6-agentic-opd-chatgpt.md)). OEL reports 20–100 OPCD steps with 64 samples per step. MAD-OPD/OPAD pays an additional debate-ensemble force-decode cost per step. SOD does not isolate teacher-scoring cost. VLA-OPD does not isolate per-step teacher-scoring or environment-reset cost in the abstract. **Lightning OPD's precomputed-logprob trick** ([wp5-industrial-systems-gemini.md](file:///Users/bytedance/Desktop/Work/on-policy-distillation-landscape/assets/research/wp5-industrial-systems-gemini.md#L28)) does not transfer cleanly to agentic settings because teacher logprobs depend on the student's not-yet-seen environment trajectory.

**Caveats:**
- 2603.x–2605.x preprints (MAD-OPD, SOD, OEL, VLA-OPD, GUI-SD) are post-knowledge-cutoff. Confidence is reproduced from prior repo audits; rows are flagged `medium` to `high` per the lead-auditor verifier ledger.
- This memo introduces **no new arXiv IDs**.

| All 2603.x–2605.x preprints (MAD-OPD, SOD, OEL, VLA-OPD, GUI-SD) | Independent re-fetch of arXiv abstract / HTML by the lead auditor; the WP9 ledger explicitly flags these IDs as "subagent-reported but not yet independently confirmed". | Re-pull each abstract; confirm Algorithm-line numbers (MAD-OPD §4.1; SOD Alg. 1; VLA-OPD Alg. 1 lines 8/12/14/20; GUI-SD §4.1–4.2); lift confidence from `medium` to `high` per row. |
