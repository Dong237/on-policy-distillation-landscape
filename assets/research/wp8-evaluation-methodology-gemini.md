## 1. Memo: evaluation philosophy

OPD claims live or die on three signals that ordinary leaderboard accuracy hides:

1. **Reduction of exposure bias on student-generated states.** OPD's central argument over offline KD is that teacher supervision on student rollouts limits compounding errors. Evaluation should include held-out tasks where the student must continue from its own intermediate states (long-CoT recovery, GUI replanning, multi-turn tool repair, long-context retrieval over self-generated drafts).
2. **Distributional fidelity to the teacher beyond accuracy.** OPD against reward-only RLVR is a distributional claim. Evaluation should report calibration (ECE, Brier), entropy, mode coverage (forward-KL gaps), perplexity on teacher distributions, and agreement-on-disagreement (where the teacher is uncertain) rather than just `pass@1`.
3. **Teacher reliability on the actually-visited state distribution.** Teacher signal quality is conditional on the student rollout. Evaluation should include teacher-vs-ground-truth audits on student-visited states and ablations where the teacher is replaced or removed.

Three operational rules follow:

- Treat accuracy-only leaderboards as **necessary but not sufficient**. Every strict-OPD claim should be paired with at least one stress-test bundle (drift, OOD, calibration, or teacher-disagreement).
- Match **adjacent baselines to method family**, not to method popularity. Pure RLVR baselines (VLM-R1, Perception-R1, R1-VL, Vision-R1, VTool-R1, ManipLVM-R1, DeepVideo-R1, SCoRe, WebRL, ReTool, Search-R1) belong next to OPD methods that share their modality and rollout horizon. Offline KD baselines (LLaVA-KD, LLAVADI, DeepSeek-R1-distill, Lion, BOND, CGD, SuperCorrect, Gemma2 pretraining KD) belong next to white-box OPD methods that share their teacher.
- **Never collapse strict and adjacent rows into the same column** in result tables. The repo's `opd_strictness` field should be carried through evaluation reports.

The benchmark bundles below are designed against this philosophy. Each bundle defines what it tests, why it separates OPD gains from RLVR gains, and which adjacent baselines must accompany the strict OPD row.

## 5. Gap map: missing benchmarks or evaluation methods

The following gaps are tracked but not added to the table because either no canonical primary source could be confirmed under WP8 rules, or the evaluation does not yet exist as a standardized public benchmark.

1. **Per-token teacher–student KL on student-visited states.** No public benchmark standardizes this. Each strict-OPD paper computes it ad-hoc. **Gap.** Recommend a small standard tool inside `implementations/evaluation.md` rather than a benchmark row.
2. **Self-error-prefix recovery.** No canonical benchmark exists. **Gap.** Recommend constructing per-domain stress sets from MATH-500, LiveCodeBench, and AppWorld by sampling the student's own incorrect prefixes and re-querying.
3. **Teacher reliability on student-visited states.** No public benchmark; this is a per-method audit. **Gap.** Recommend that the strictness-evidence field in `tables/opd_papers.md` be extended with `teacher_audit_done` (yes/no).
4. **Long-horizon GUI / browser strict OPD eval.** OSWorld, WebArena, AndroidWorld, WindowsAgentArena, AssistantBench cover long-horizon agent quality, but none is OPD-specific. **Gap.** OK as adjacent control; do not promote as OPD-defining.
5. **Diffusion/flow OPD trajectory-fidelity benchmark.** No public benchmark scores per-step velocity-field alignment between student and teacher. **Gap.** Track inside the Diffusion-Flow-ImageGen bundle as method-internal metric only.
6. **Cross-tokenizer OPD alignment benchmark.** No public benchmark. **Gap.** Same-tokenizer reference run is the de-facto standard.
7. **Speech-LLM OPD-specific eval.** Big Bench Audio, MMAU, AIR-Bench cover speech-LLM reasoning but not OPD-specific. X-OPD is the only strict speech OPD seed. **Gap.** Acceptable; track text-LLM teacher upper bound as control.
8. **VLA OPD-specific OOD recovery.** RoboCasa, SimplerEnv help, but VLA-OPD recovery-after-failure is not standardized. **Gap.** Recommend recording recovery-rate from method papers as method-internal metric.
9. **Calibration on student-visited states for VLM.** No public VLM-specific calibration benchmark. **Gap.** Use HallusionBench / POPE / MMHal-Bench refusal correctness as proxy.
10. **Industrial teacher-serving cost transparency.** Vendor reports do not standardize teacher-cost share. **Gap.** Recommend `notes/teacher-reliability.md` extension with a teacher-serving-cost field (optional).
11. **Multi-turn agentic OPD long-horizon credit assignment.** SWE-bench Verified, AppWorld, τ-Bench cover task success, but none gives per-step OPD vs RLVR credit split. **Gap.** Recommend ablation-based decomposition (OPD-Hybrid Decomposition bundle).
12. **Rolling, OPD-aware contamination-controlled benchmark.** LiveCodeBench / LiveBench refresh problems but do not certify OPD-relevance. **Gap.** Use existing rolling benchmarks; do not propose new rows.
13. **Benchmark for OPD on continual learning.** SDFT-Continual cites ToolAlpaca, SciKnowEval, medical QA — no single canonical CL-OPD benchmark exists. TRACE is the closest. **Gap.** Track TRACE as candidate, but no row added because primary source verification is partial.
14. **Benchmark for privileged-context OPD (OPCD/OPSDL).** No standardized benchmark; methods construct task-specific privileged-vs-unprivileged splits. **Gap.** Method-internal metric only.
15. **Benchmark for verbal-feedback OPD (OVD, FCP, ALT).** No standardized benchmark beyond AlpacaEval-2 / MT-Bench. **Gap.** Use BlackBox-API-Alignment bundle.

## 8. Decisions answered

- **Which benchmark bundles best separate OPD gains from reward-only RLVR gains?** LLM-Calibration-Uncertainty, LLM-RolloutDrift-OOD, OPD-RL-Hybrid-Decomposition, VLM-Perception-Stress, Video-Temporal-Grounding, Diffusion-Flow-ImageGen. All four require either calibration / fidelity metrics or a three-way ablation that RLVR-only cannot match.
- **Which benchmarks actually test exposure-bias reduction or recovery from self-generated errors?** LLM-RolloutDrift-OOD (LiveCodeBench, LiveBench, OlympiadBench post-cutoff, USACO post-cutoff), Agentic-Tool-Code (SWE-bench Verified, AppWorld, τ-Bench), GUI-Agent-LongHorizon (OSWorld, AndroidWorld, WebArena), VLA-Robotics (LIBERO long-horizon, RoboCasa OOD, SimplerEnv).
- **Which metrics capture teacher uncertainty preservation, calibration, entropy, and distributional fidelity?** Distributional Fidelity (per-token KL/JS/TV, top-k overlap), Calibration (ECE, Brier, selective accuracy), Entropy & Uncertainty Preservation (entropy histogram KL, Spearman teacher–student entropy), Mode Coverage (pass@k − pass@1).
- **Which benchmark families are contaminated, private, brittle, or too accuracy-only for OPD claims?** Heavily contaminated: MATH, GSM8K, MT-Bench, MMLU, AlpacaEval (without LC), MathVista. Brittle / saturating: LIBERO defaults, AIME (n=30). Private: GPQA-Diamond partly, RealWorldQA, OSWorld test pool, SWE-bench full. Accuracy-only and weak for OPD claims: MMMU-Pro by itself, LogicVista, OCRBench. Use them only inside bundles that pair them with calibration / drift / stress controls.
- **How should evaluation differ for token/logit OPD, response-level black-box OPD, privileged self-OPD, action-token OPD, and diffusion/flow borderline OPD?** See the family table in §6. Headline: token/logit OPD requires distributional-fidelity metrics; black-box OPD must rely on multi-judge response-level metrics; privileged self-OPD requires iteration-level monotonicity and backward transfer; action-token OPD requires long-horizon success and recovery; diffusion/flow OPD requires per-step velocity-field MSE plus image-quality.
- **What adjacent baselines should accompany each strict OPD family?** See §7.
- **What benchmark rows should be added or updated in `tables/benchmarks.md`?** See §4 for the proposed additions and updates.

## 9. Decision rules applied

- No method's strictness label is changed. All references match the labels currently in `tables/opd_papers.md` and `tables/vlm_opd_papers.md`.
- Benchmark popularity (e.g., MATH, GSM8K, MMLU, MT-Bench) is not used as evidence of OPD relevance — these are flagged contamination-risk inside their bundles.
- Bundles privilege OPD-specific stress tests: rollout drift (LiveCodeBench, LiveBench), OOD prompts (LiveBench, GPQA-Diamond, AIME post-cutoff, OlympiadBench post-cutoff), long-horizon credit assignment (SWE-bench Verified, OSWorld, WebArena, LIBERO long, MAD), calibration / entropy (TruthfulQA, SimpleQA, NQ confidence, MathVerse), teacher disagreement (HallusionBench, MMHal-Bench, perception-vs-reasoning splits), and self-generated error recovery (self-error-prefix continuation, recovery rate).
- Strict-OPD methods and adjacent controls are kept in separate columns in §2 and §7.
- Where a benchmark source could not be verified at primary-source level, it is listed in §5 (Gap map) as "not found" rather than added to §4.
