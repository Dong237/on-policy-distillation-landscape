## Memo

Of the 15 assigned methods, 10 are locked `strict_opd` against verified primary quotes, 3 `borderline_strict`, and 2 `partial_opd`. No new candidate papers are introduced; a small number of name collisions are flagged.

**Strict OPD (C1+C2+C3 all explicit):** Uni-OPD, MAD-OPD, VOLD (Stage 2 only), MiMo-V2-Flash (MOPD stage), G-OPD, PACED (reverse-KL self-distillation track only), SDPO, OPCD, SCOPE (incorrect-path branch only), KDRL (KD-RKL subcomponent).

**Borderline strict:** REOPOLD (teacher log-ratio consumed as token-level reward with clipping rather than pure same-rollout KL), Gemma 2 post-training (single sentence in §4; no explicit loss in the report), OEL (strict only for the OPCD consolidation substage).

**Partial OPD:** Lightning OPD (teacher logprobs are precomputed over SFT rollouts, so C1 fails freshness), DistiLLM-2 (paper explicitly says "batch approach … rather than on-policy"; SGOs drawn from θ_{e-1}).

**Name-collision flags (no new papers introduced):**
- **SDPO** collides with Segment-Level DPO (arXiv 2501.01821) and stepwise DPO (arXiv 2403.19270); intended paper is "Self-Distillation Preference Optimization" / "Reinforcement Learning via Self-Distillation" (arXiv 2601.20802).
- **PACED** collides with "Paced-Curriculum Distillation" (arXiv 2302.01049, medical imaging); intended OPD paper is arXiv 2603.11178.
- **OEL** has no canonical OPD acronym; matched to "Online Experiential Learning" (arXiv 2603.16856).
- **SCOPE** matched to arXiv 2604.10688 (Signal-Calibrated On-Policy distillation Enhancement), not the speculative full-name expansion.

**Caveats:** Several papers are post knowledge-cutoff preprints (2603.x–2605.x). Where the published abstract/HTML did not expose the loss equation, confidence was downgraded rather than inferred. Labels are scoped to the qualifying training stage when a method is pipelined.
