# Audit of On Policy Distillation Landscape Candidates

## Memo of major findings

The clearest **strict_opd** cases are the methods whose primary sources explicitly say that the **student/current policy** generates the rollout, a **teacher or privileged-context teacher** evaluates that **same rollout**, and the **training objective** uses a **token-level divergence or equivalent dense supervision term**. In this audit, the highest-confidence examples are **MiniLLM**, **Fast OPD**, **Entropy-Aware OPD**, **Veto**, **OPSD**, **OPSDC/CRISP**, **Nemotron-Cascade2**, **Video-OPD**, **X-OPD**, **VLA-OPD**, **MAD-OPD**, and **OPCD**. citeturn17view0turn17view4turn10search2turn11search0turn15view2turn19search0turn19search1turn33view0turn37view1turn14view13turn14view14turn36view0turn19search2

A second cluster is genuinely **on-policy-like** but should be **downgraded** because it fails at least one strict condition. The most common failure mode is **C3**: the method uses **scalar rewards / discriminator scores / verbal trajectory scores / preference-style signals** rather than dense teacher supervision on the exact rollout. That is why **GAD**, **OVD**, **PRISM**, and **RLKD** are not strict under your rubric, even though they are on-policy or distillation-adjacent. citeturn32view1turn32view0turn34view0turn34view2turn29view3

A third cluster is **mixed or under-documented**. **Qwen3’s OPD stage** and **Gemma 2 post-training** are called distillation or on-policy in official reports, but the public primary descriptions do not expose enough C1/C2/C3 detail to keep them as strict without further internal documentation. Several names also appear to be **name collisions or non-OPD methods**: **DistillDirect** is real but is on-policy **DPO-style** distillation rather than strict OPD; **DBKD** is real but is ordinary decision-based KD; **TED** is real but **training-free**; and “**SCOPE as Self-play Contrastive On-Policy Evaluation**” was not verified as a real primary-source method and appears to be a collision with the actual **SCOPE** paper. citeturn24view0turn24view1turn27view1turn27view3turn30search8turn30search13turn30search2turn30search6turn11search2
