Memo: This WP1 redirect spot-check does not reopen the WP2 black-box primary queue. The five audited methods all belong outside black-box/API OPD because they require teacher logits, token probabilities, or teacher/reference distributions. Under the repo strict OPD rule, PAD is the only clean strict row at the method-core level: its PPD path samples responses from the student, scores those same responses with teacher/student probability-derived rewards, and aligns full preference distributions with JSD. ADPA has a strong on-policy white-box subcomponent, but the method-level row is safer as `borderline_strict` because the objective is advantage-guided and mixed with SFT/offline preference-alignment machinery rather than a pure same-rollout KL-style distillation loop. GAKD remains adjacent because its reverse-KL/adversarial evidence points to teacher-generated or corpus sequences rather than current student rollouts. daDPO is partial because it uses sampled teacher/student response pairs plus white-box teacher distribution in a DPO-style objective, but the pairs are static/off-policy for the update. CTPD is adjacent: it solves cross-tokenizer white-box preference projection over static preference data, with no current student rollout loop found.
