## 1. Memo

**Strict OPD (multimodal frontier):**
- **VOLD** — VLM student generates on-policy reasoning traces; text-only LLM teacher (Qwen3) supervises those same traces with token-level KL alongside GRPO reward. Strict for the unified RL-OPD stage; the SFT cold-start is not strict.
- **Video-OPD** — current-policy temporal-video-grounding trajectories sampled from the MLLM student; frontier multimodal teacher (Qwen2.5-VL-72B class) supervises those exact trajectories via reverse KL on grounding-token logits. Strongest current strict-OPD seed in temporal video grounding.
- **X-OPD** — speech-LLM student rollouts are evaluated by a text teacher LLM that produces token-level cross-modal feedback on the speech-LLM's own outputs. Strict cross-modal OPD seed; only modality boundary item.
- **Uni-OPD** — single- or multi-teacher token KL with margin-calibration on student rollouts across LLM and MLLM benchmarks. Strict at the dual-perspective MLLM level.
- **VLA-OPD** — student VLA policy collects on-policy trajectories; frozen expert teacher labels those visited states with action-token logits; reverse-KL action-token objective. Strict in the embodied / action-token sense (already line-audited in [wp9-line-audit-vla-prism-gemini.md](file:///Users/bytedance/Desktop/Work/on-policy-distillation-landscape/assets/research/wp9-line-audit-vla-prism-gemini.md#L31)).
- **LiteGUI** — guided OPD / GKD stage on student-generated GUI sequences with oracle-context teacher reverse-KL on action tokens. Strict only for the Stage-1 guided OPD; Stage-2 multi-solution GRPO is RL-adjacent.

**Strict OPD (limited / single-step):**
- **GUI-SD** — privileged-visual self-teacher supplies a weighted reverse-KL on the student's own coordinate-output tokens. Strict for **GUI grounding** only; not multi-step GUI control.

**Borderline strict:**
- **PRISM** — current-policy multimodal responses scored by an MoE discriminator with disentangled perception / reasoning experts (no teacher logits). Stronger than scalar reward, but supervision is response-level + adversarial → fails strict C3 (already line-audited).

**Partial OPD:**
- (No queue item lands cleanly here at the method level after primary-source check; PRISM is the closest neighbor and is held at borderline.)

**Adjacent (OPD-related but C3 fails):**
- **VLM-R1** — student rollouts + GRPO on verifier reward. No teacher distribution.
- **Perception-R1** — verifier-reward GRPO on visual perception tasks.
- **R1-VL / StepGRPO** — step-wise advantages from verifier; no teacher distillation distribution on the student's tokens.
- **Vision-R1** — RLVR on multimodal reasoning; no teacher KL.
- **DeepVideo-R1** — RLVR on video reasoning.
- **VTool-R1** — RL with tool-use rewards on student multimodal trajectories.
- **OpenVLThinker** — iterative self-improvement with verifier-reward RL; teacher rationales are used in cold-start SFT only (off-policy), so the on-policy stage is reward-only.

**Not OPD (offline KD / static synthetic supervision):**
- **LLaVA-KD** — token + relation KD on a fixed teacher-supervised dataset; teacher does not supervise current student rollouts.
- **LLAVADI** — small MLLM logit-distillation pipeline; offline KD on pre-collected supervision.
- **Visual Program Distillation (VPD)** — distills program traces into a VLM via SFT on teacher-generated programs (the student does not re-execute and receive teacher-distribution supervision on its own outputs).

**Industrial / unclear (held out without primary §-level disclosure):**
- **Qwen3-VL** — public materials confirm an OPD-style reasoning distillation on the LLM backbone; the VL public report does not expose the multimodal OPD loss form. Hold at `borderline_strict_industrial` for the multimodal stage.
- **InternVL3.5** — official report features MPO and VisualPRM-RL; does not expose strict OPD loss on the VLM student. Adjacent at the public level.
- **Gemma 3** — model card describes multimodal capabilities; the public report does not expose a strict OPD loss (Gemma 2 post-training is itself only `borderline_strict` per [tables/opd_papers.md](file:///Users/bytedance/Desktop/Work/on-policy-distillation-landscape/tables/opd_papers.md#L67)). Hold at `borderline_strict_industrial` if the multimodal stage inherits the LLM OPD; else adjacent.
- **Gemini** — closed; no §-level OPD exposure. Hold out of strict.
- **MiniCPM-V** — multimodal SFT + RLAIF / DPO pipeline; no public strict OPD loss form. Adjacent.

**Caveats:**
- Many 2602.x–2605.x preprints are post-knowledge-cutoff; quotes are reproduced from existing line audits in this repo ([wp9-line-audit-vla-prism-gemini.md](file:///Users/bytedance/Desktop/Work/on-policy-distillation-landscape/assets/research/wp9-line-audit-vla-prism-gemini.md), [wp6-agentic-candidate-line-audit-gemini.md](file:///Users/bytedance/Desktop/Work/on-policy-distillation-landscape/assets/research/wp6-agentic-candidate-line-audit-gemini.md)) and [tables/opd_papers.md](file:///Users/bytedance/Desktop/Work/on-policy-distillation-landscape/tables/opd_papers.md).
