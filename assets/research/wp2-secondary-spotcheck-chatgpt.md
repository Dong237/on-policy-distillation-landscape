Memo: The secondary WP2 spot-check adds no strict black-box/API OPD seed. The highest-priority items mostly exit WP2 because they require white-box teacher distributions or logits: GAKD, ADPA, daDPO, PAD, and CTPD. RLKD and GAR are useful on-policy RL neighbors, but their teacher or discriminator signal is consumed as scalar or step reward. RLTF, FCP, and ALT are the closest black-box/API verbal-feedback neighbors because they use current or iterative policy samples plus text feedback, but their objectives compile feedback into self-distilled generations, feedback prediction, or conditional SFT rather than dense teacher-style supervision on the exact rollout.
