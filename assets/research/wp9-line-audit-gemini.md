# WP9 Line-Level OPD Audit (Gemini)

## Memo

I audited the 15 assigned methods against primary sources (arXiv abstracts/HTML/PDFs and one official Xiaomi tech report). Twelve resolved to verifiable primary sources with enough evidence to lock a label; three are weaker:

- **Strict OPD (C1+C2+C3 explicitly satisfied):** Uni-OPD, MAD-OPD, VOLD (Stage 2), MiMo-V2-Flash (MOPD), G-OPD, PACED, SDPO, OPCD, OEL (consolidation substage), SCOPE (incorrect-path branch), KDRL.
- **Borderline strict:** REOPOLD (teacher signal consumed as token-level reward, not pure KL), Gemma 2 post-training (only one sentence + cite, no explicit objective in the report), Lightning OPD (rollouts are precomputed offline SFT rollouts → C1 fails strict freshness).
- **Partial OPD:** DistiLLM-2 (mixed on/off rollouts; contrastive over TGO/SGO rather than same-rollout teacher KL).

**Name-collision flags:**
- **SDPO** collides with Segment-Level DPO (arXiv 2501.01821) and stepwise DPO (arXiv 2403.19270); intended OPD paper is "Reinforcement Learning via Self-Distillation" (arXiv 2601.20802).
- **PACED** collides with "Paced-Curriculum Distillation" (arXiv 2302.01049, medical imaging); intended paper is arXiv 2603.11178.
- **OEL** has no canonical OPD acronym; matched to "Online Experiential Learning" (arXiv 2603.16856).
- **SCOPE** is the real arXiv 2604.10688 ("Signal-Calibrated On-Policy distillation Enhancement"), not the speculative "Self-play Contrastive On-Policy Evaluation" expansion.

**Caveats:** Several papers are post knowledge-cutoff preprints (2603.x–2605.x range); evidence here is from web-fetched arXiv abstracts/HTML, not internal verification of every objective equation. Where the abstract did not expose the loss equation, confidence was downgraded rather than inferred.
