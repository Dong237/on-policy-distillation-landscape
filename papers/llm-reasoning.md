# LLM Reasoning OPD

This page tracks OPD methods for text-only reasoning, math, code, and agentic language tasks.

## Inclusion criteria

- LLM student generates its own reasoning traces or answers during training.
- Teacher or expert feedback is applied to those traces.
- The method targets reasoning, long-CoT, math, code, or agentic language behavior.

## Seed families

- GKD / generalized knowledge distillation.
- MiniLLM / reverse-KL OPD.
- Black-box OPD and discriminator-based variants.
- OPD + RLVR hybrids.
- Multi-teacher OPD.
