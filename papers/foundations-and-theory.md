This page tracks the theory and motivating frame of OPD: exposure bias, student state visitation, interactive imitation learning, f-divergence views, and the shift from off-policy data to student on-policy trajectories.

## Include

- OPD survey and position papers.
- GKD and MiniLLM-style foundational methods.
