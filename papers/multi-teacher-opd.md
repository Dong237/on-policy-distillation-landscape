# Multi-Teacher OPD

Multi-teacher OPD consolidates feedback from several teacher models, experts, or debate participants.

## Inclusion criteria

- Student generates on-policy states.
- Multiple teachers provide token-level, sequence-level, or debated supervision.
- The method specifies how teacher conflict is resolved.
