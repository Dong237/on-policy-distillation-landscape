# Surveys and Position Papers

This page tracks surveys and position papers that define or organize OPD and related distillation methods.

## Inclusion criteria

- Provides a taxonomy, theory, or broad survey of OPD, KD, RLVR, or multimodal post-training.
- Helps classify methods by rollout source, teacher access, or supervision signal.

## Seed entries

### A Survey of On-Policy Distillation for Large Language Models

- **Year:** 2026
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00626
- **Modality:** LLM
- **Strictness:** survey
- **Why it matters:** Defines OPD around student self-generated trajectories and teacher feedback, and organizes the field by feedback signal, teacher access, and loss granularity.
